Sync with my other PCs

    
Keeps your printers, filaments, processes and app settings the same on every PC, through a
      folder your cloud drive already syncs (OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox). No account needed: pick the same folder
      on each PC. The newer change wins; a preset you delete is deleted on the other PCs too.

    

    

      

    

    
Printer connections (IP, access code) and the camera login are synced too, so keep the
      folder private. The app checks for changes every minute while it is open.
